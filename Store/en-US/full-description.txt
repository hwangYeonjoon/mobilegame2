Turn broken machines into your next robot.

Fight through a ruined industrial world, recover parts from mechanical enemies, and assemble a robot you can pilot in battle. Choose the machine you want to build, then hunt for its parts.

BUILD YOUR WAY OUT
Collect Cannon, Drive and Armor parts. Assemble an Artillery Tank, Assault Walker or Bulwark Guardian, then combine primary and support parts to change how your robot fights. Discover designs in the Robot Codex and display the robots you have built.

MOVE, SURVIVE, EVOLVE
Move with one hand while your Guardian attacks automatically. Choose skills, evolve your weapons, and decide when to board your robot. Short battles bring waves of enemies, warning zones and bosses.

60 OPERATIONS ACROSS 10 REGIONS
Explore the Abandoned Railway, Cooling Facility, Deep Furnace, Orbital Assembly Port and more. Each region has its own mechanical enemies, hazards and materials to recover.

GROW AT YOUR PACE
Upgrade Guardians and equipment with Gold and Common Parts. Earn materials through play or choose optional supplies to save time. Practice difficult encounters and review recent damage when you need a different approach.

MAKE YOUR SQUAD YOUR OWN
Collect outfits, robot styles, frames and display themes. Preview cosmetics before choosing them, use them in battle, and show your collection in your Showcase. Cosmetic outfits do not raise combat stats.

KEEP BUILDING
Take on weekly robot challenges, compare records, and call on a guild member's support Guardian. The Equal Gear Trial uses fixed combat stats, so purchased equipment upgrades do not affect that competition.
