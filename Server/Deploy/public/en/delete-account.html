ACCOUNT & DATA

Delete your Scrap Guard account

Delete your server account and related data with your personal recovery code, even without the app.

Deletion cannot be undone.
Progress, currencies, equipment, outfits, server purchase entitlements, public profile, rankings, guild membership and Google linking will be deleted. Deletion does not submit a refund request.

Delete on this website

You need the 43-character personal recovery code from Account & Save in the app. Do not enter your public ID or Google password.

Your code is sent to this server over an encrypted connection. It is not placed in a URL or browser storage.

Lost your recovery code?

If you linked a Google account, recover your account through Google in the app, then delete it. If you cannot use the app, email an account deletion request with your game public ID. We will verify ownership before processing the request. Never email passwords or recovery codes.

Information retained after deletion

Purchase-token hashes and transaction statuses with the account link removed are retained for the lifetime of the service to prevent duplicate grants and refund abuse. Encrypted original tokens are removed. Backups expire within 30 days. Google's payment and account records, and local trial saves on your device, are separate. See our Privacy Policy for details.
