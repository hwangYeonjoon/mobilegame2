Privacy Policy

MrStudio processes the information below to provide accounts, saved progress, purchase verification, ad rewards and support for the Android game Scrap Guard (io.github.hwangyeonjoon.scrapguard). Local trial saves and server accounts are stored separately.

1. Information and purposes

CategoryInformation and use
Server accountRandom public ID, recovery-code hash, creation time, game progress, currencies, equipment and transaction records: authentication, saving and recovery, reward delivery and abuse prevention.
Google account linking (optional)We verify the ID token issued by Google and store a hash of the account's unique identifier. We do not store your name, profile photo or email address in the game database. We do not receive your Google password.
Public profileDisplay name, guardian, equipment, outfits, showcase and ranking records, guild, cheers, blocks and reports: in-game social features. Other players can see your public ID and showcase information.
Google Play purchasesProduct ID, encrypted purchase token and token hash, purchase time, grant and refund status: purchase verification, duplicate-grant prevention, restoration and refunds. Google handles card numbers and payment passwords; our game server does not receive them.
Rewarded adsReward claim ID, game public ID, ad unit and transaction IDs, time and reward status: ad verification and duplicate-grant prevention. When serving ads, the Google AdMob SDK may process IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, app interactions and diagnostics.
Game operations statisticsGem acquisition and spending, outfit purchases and equipment, and active dates: game economy and service operations. Trials of outfits you do not own are recorded only if you separately opt in within the app.
Connections and supportIP addresses are used for request limits and security. Diagnostic logs and support emails, message contents and attachments are used for troubleshooting and customer support.

2. Your choices

Google linking, watching rewarded ads and outfit-trial analytics are optional. You can withdraw consent for outfit-trial analytics in settings; the related trial records are then deleted. Where the app offers ad privacy options, you can change your choices in the ad privacy menu in settings. Declining an optional feature does not prevent use of the other game features.

3. Service providers and international processing

The game server and database are hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) Lightsail in Seoul, South Korea. Cloudflare provides domain and DNS services; the game API currently uses DNS-only routing. Google sign-in, Google Play payments and Google AdMob ads connect to Google services, and identifiers needed for those features are sent to Google. Google may process information in the United States and other countries.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Account data, progress, Google links, social data and individual game statistics are removed from the active database when the account is deleted.
  • Optional outfit-trial records and active-date records are kept for up to 90 days and are also removed when the account is deleted.
  • After account deletion, hashes of processed purchase tokens and product, grant and refund statuses with the account link removed are retained for the lifetime of the service to prevent duplicate grants and refund abuse. The encrypted original purchase token is removed. We do not treat the retained information as fully anonymous.
  • Server backups rotate and expire within 30 days. Information remaining in backups is restricted to disaster recovery and is deleted when the backup expires.
  • Support emails are retained for up to three years after the case closes. If a separate legal retention obligation applies, we will explain its reason and duration. Server diagnostic logs rotate according to file-size limits.

5. Access, correction and deletion requests

Use Account & Save → Delete server account in the app, or delete your account on this website using your personal recovery code. The website works without installing the app. If you do not have your code, or need access to or correction of your personal information, contact duswnsghkd@gmail.com. We verify ownership; a public ID alone cannot authorize deletion of someone else's account.

To delete only outfit-trial records while keeping your account, turn off outfit-trial analytics consent in settings. These records are removed from the active database. To request deletion of other specific data, email support with your game public ID and the items you want deleted. Do not email your recovery code or Google password. After verifying ownership, we will explain the outcome and any information that must be retained, including its retention period. Backups containing deleted data expire within 30 days.

6. Security and intended audience

We use HTTPS encryption in transit, restricted server and administrator access, hashed recovery codes and encrypted purchase tokens. The game is intended for users aged 13 and above, and is not directed to children under 13. Google account and advertising age and consent requirements depend on the service settings and region.

7. Contact and changes

Privacy contact: MrStudio / duswnsghkd@gmail.com.
Material changes will be announced on this page and through in-app notices.
