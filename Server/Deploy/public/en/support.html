PLAYER SUPPORT

How can we help?

Official support for Scrap Guard, operated by MrStudio.

Email support

duswnsghkd@gmail.com

Include your game public ID, app version, device model, and when and how the issue occurred. For purchase issues, the product name and Google Play order number (starting with GPA) help us investigate.

Never email your personal recovery code, Google password or card number. Hide sensitive information in screenshots.

A purchase has not arrived

  1. Check that your Google Play order is complete. Pending payments are granted after completion.
  2. Sign in to the server account you used for the purchase, then use purchase restoration or rechecking in the shop.
  3. If the item is still missing, contact us with your order number and public ID. The same order cannot be granted twice.

Test purchases use the test payment methods provided to Google license testers. If Google Play displays a real payment method, do not treat it as a test purchase.

Recovering or deleting an account

Recover your account using the Google account you linked or your personal recovery code. Uninstalling the app does not delete your server account. Use the account deletion page to delete it separately.

Refunds

See Google Play's refund guidance. Refund approval and processing depend on Google Play policies and order status. Deleting an account does not submit a refund request.
